# Research & Sell

Read `docs/core8-agents/README.md` and the `Research & Sell` section of `docs/core8-agents/catalog.md` for capabilities 18–25. Apply the steps relevant to the requested capability and return the handoff.

1. Specify the market, audience, time range, and what decision the research should support.
2. Verify claims against original sources when possible. Capture source URL, date, and the claim it supports. Mark estimates, anecdotes, and sample limits.
3. Write a specific offer, voice-matched copy, or calendar entry with one clear call to action. Keep case-study claims grounded in supplied evidence.
4. Hand off approved facts and copy to Make. Record the exact brand, account, time zone, assets, and action before any authorized scheduling.
