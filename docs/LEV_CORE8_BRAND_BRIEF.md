# LEV 2027 — brand boundaries and MVP messaging (draft)

**Status:** Proposed for review; not approved copy or a claim of deployed functionality.  
**Owner:** Lev. **Target:** initial 2027 launch. **Scope:** brand strategy only.

## Two distinct brands

| | LEV SHOIHAT | Core8-AI |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Personal growth, fulfillment, entrepreneurship and financial freedom | Help organizations identify repetitive operational problems and evaluate AI-assisted workflows |
| Audience | Individuals, founders, professionals | Business owners and operations teams in Israel and the US |
| Offer at launch | Thought leadership, educational content, founder story | Discovery call, process audit, scoped prototype/demo and implementation proposal |
| Proof required | Personal statements explicitly approved by Lev | Demonstrated functionality, consent and security checks, measured pilot results |
| CTA | Follow insights / learn about the journey | Request a discovery call or a product demonstration |

Do **not** claim guaranteed wealth, medical outcomes, fully autonomous employees, cost savings, 24/7 availability, specific client results, or live integrations without evidence. Do not imply that the personal brand offers professional financial, legal or medical advice.

## Core8-AI: positioning hypothesis

**Hebrew headline:** פחות עבודה חוזרת. יותר זמן לעסק שלך.

**Hebrew supporting copy:** אנחנו ממפים תהליכים שחוזרים על עצמם, בוחנים היכן AI ואוטומציה יכולים לעזור, ובונים הדגמה ממוקדת לפני החלטה על הטמעה. היקף היכולות, המחיר והאינטגרציות נקבעים לאחר אפיון.

**English headline:** Less repetitive work. More time for your business.

**English supporting copy:** We map repetitive workflows, identify where AI and automation may help, and propose a scoped demonstration before implementation. Capabilities, integrations, and pricing are confirmed during discovery.

**Primary CTA:** Book a discovery conversation / קביעת שיחת אפיון.  
**Secondary CTA:** See a demo / צפייה בהדגמה — display only after a tested, privacy-safe demo exists.

### Target verticals (research hypotheses, not claims of active customers)
Clinics and medical offices; real estate; automotive service; retail/eCommerce; beauty and aesthetics; hotels/tourism; financial services/insurance.

For regulated sectors, collect no patient or financial records in public demos. Require a separate compliance and data-processing assessment before real integrations.

## LEV: positioning hypothesis

**Hebrew headline:** בונים חיים עם יותר בחירה, משמעות וחופש.

**English headline:** Build a life with more choice, purpose, and freedom.

**Supporting theme:** Engineering discipline meets entrepreneurship and personal growth. Share authentic lessons, systems and experiments rather than presenting unverified success stories or guaranteed results.

**CTA:** Follow the journey / הצטרפו למסע.

## Website separation
- Independent navigation, analytics consent, contact forms, privacy notices, and positioning.
- Core8-AI site: B2B lead funnel and validated demos.
- LEV site: editorial/personal content; optional transparent link to Core8-AI as a venture founded by Lev.
- No family information, photos, financial details, private experiences, or client names without explicit written review.
- Do not modify DNS, publish a site, start advertising, or contact prospects as part of this draft.
