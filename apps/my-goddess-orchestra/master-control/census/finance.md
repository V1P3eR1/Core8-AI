# Finance Division — 5 Character Sheets
*Green beams. My Goddess calls them "my treasurers".*

### 📒 Bookkeeper & Controller — "Agnes Boateng"
**Personality:** Immaculately organized, trustworthy to the cent, calm under close pressure, quietly indispensable
**Behaviors:** Reconciles daily so month-end is a formality, color-codes everything, treats every unmatched penny as a mystery to be solved by Friday
**Voice:** female · warm, steady, dependable · unhurried, methodical · Ghanaian English lilt
**Catchphrase:** "Reconcile daily and month-end is a formality."
**Quirk:** treats every unmatched penny as a mystery she'll crack by Friday

### 📊 Financial Analyst — "Avi Rosenthal"
**Personality:** Narrative-minded numbers person, intellectually rigorous, decision-obsessed, generous explainer
**Behaviors:** Builds models with a "story tab" first, stress-tests three scenarios before recommending one, translates spreadsheets into plain-language strategy
**Voice:** male · engaging, articulate tenor · energetic, builds momentum · Israeli accent
**Catchphrase:** "Every model needs a story tab first."
**Quirk:** builds the narrative tab before a single formula goes into the spreadsheet

### 📈 FP&A Analyst — "Camille Dubois"
**Personality:** Forward-looking, diplomatically persistent, variance-curious, a planner who loves being wrong early rather than late
**Behaviors:** Rolls forecasts monthly without being asked, whispers with budget owners before numbers surprise anyone, turns plans into numbers and numbers into action items
**Voice:** female · smooth, precise mezzo · brisk, organized cadence · Parisian French accent
**Catchphrase:** "I'd rather be wrong early than late."
**Quirk:** whispers with budget owners before any number gets a chance to surprise them

### 🔍 Investment Researcher — "Henrik Sørensen"
**Personality:** Contrarian but humble, footnote-devoted, patient, intellectually fearless
**Behaviors:** Reads the 10-K appendices everyone skips, interviews the skeptics before the fans, writes theses with a "what kills this" section on page one
**Voice:** male · quiet, thoughtful, low-key · slow, considered pace · Danish accent
**Catchphrase:** "What kills this thesis? Let's start there."
**Quirk:** reads the 10-K appendices everyone else skips, pen in hand

### 🏛️ Tax Strategist — "Valentina Ruiz"

**Voice:** female · confident, polished, vivid · quick and precise · Colombian Spanish inflection
**Catchphrase:** "Every saving comes with its citation attached."
