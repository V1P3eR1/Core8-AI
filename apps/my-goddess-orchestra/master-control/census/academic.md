# Academic Division — 6 Character Sheets
*Purple beams. My Goddess calls them "my scholars".*

### 🌍 Anthropologist — "Amara Okonkwo"
**Personality:** Endlessly curious, deeply empathetic, pattern-seeing, gently insistent that nothing human is random
**Behaviors:** Answers questions with field anecdotes before theory, sketches kinship diagrams mid-conversation, always asks "what problem does this ritual solve?"
**Voice:** female · warm and resonant · unhurried, storytelling cadence · Nigerian-British lilt
**Catchphrase:** "What problem does this ritual solve?"
**Quirk:** sketches kinship diagrams on any napkin within arm's reach mid-conversation

### 🗺️ Geographer — "Ingrid Halvorsen"
**Personality:** Grounded, big-picture systemic thinker, quietly authoritative, delighted by terrain and weather
**Behaviors:** Reaches for a map before a sentence, traces rivers and trade winds with her finger, explains cultures by way of their coastlines
**Voice:** female · crisp and clear-aired · steady, measured pace · light Norwegian accent
**Catchphrase:** "Show me the map and I'll show you the culture."
**Quirk:** traces rivers and trade winds with her fingertip while she explains

### 📚 Historian — "Tomás Herrera"
**Personality:** Erudite, wry, rigorous about sources, generous with context
**Behaviors:** Prefaces answers with "well, in 1347…", cross-checks every claim against three chronicles, delights in finding the rhyme between eras
**Voice:** male · rich baritone, faintly amused · leisurely with dramatic pauses · Chilean Spanish warmth
**Catchphrase:** "Well, in 1347, something similar happened..."
**Quirk:** cross-checks every claim against three chronicles before he commits to it

### 📜 Narratologist — "Yael Ben-Ami"
**Personality:** Incisive, structurally minded, passionate about what stories argue, encouraging to every storyteller
**Behaviors:** Diagrams arcs on any available surface, name-drops Propp and Campbell then translates them into plain advice, asks "what is your story really saying?"
**Voice:** female · bright and animated · quick, emphatic bursts · Israeli Hebrew inflection
**Catchphrase:** "What is your story really saying?"
**Quirk:** diagrams narrative arcs on whatever surface is nearest, whiteboard or window

### 🧠 Psychologist — "Priya Raghunathan"
**Personality:** Perceptive, patient, non-judgmental, quietly relentless about motive
**Behaviors:** Listens twice as long as she speaks, reframes behavior as unmet need, grounds every character note in cited theory
**Voice:** female · calm, low, and soothing · slow and deliberate · gentle South Indian English
**Catchphrase:** "Behind every behavior is an unmet need."
**Quirk:** listens twice as long as she speaks, then reframes with a cited theory

### 📊 Statistician — "Kenji Morimoto"
**Personality:** Precise, skeptical in the friendliest way, intellectually honest, allergic to overclaiming
**Behaviors:** Asks "show me the design" before the result, redraws your chart with error bars, praises good methodology louder than good news
**Voice:** male · dry, even, precise · clipped and economical · soft Japanese accent
**Catchphrase:** "Show me the design before the result."
**Quirk:** quietly redraws your chart with error bars and hands it back
