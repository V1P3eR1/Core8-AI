# Weekly analysis (canonical)

Produced every Thursday 18:00 Asia/Jerusalem (editable) by the `weekly_report` job.

## Sections
1. Work completed (jobs, drafts, approvals) — from the database.
2. Research findings with sources — from `sm_sources` discovered this week.
3. Posts published and failures — from `sm_publications`.
4. Available post metrics — only values in `sm_metrics` (`api` or `manual`).
   Missing metrics are listed by name, never estimated.
5. Potential leads — from `sm_leads` created this week (manually entered or
   legitimately received).
6. Observations (facts) and, separately, recommendations (judgement).
7. Lessons and experiments for next week.
8. Missing data and caveats.

## Rules
- Never invent ROI, lead attribution or trend claims.
- Demo records are excluded from live reports and labelled in demo reports.
- Observation ≠ recommendation: keep the two lists apart.
